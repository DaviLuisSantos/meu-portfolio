A single-page site with the brand manifesto and email capture for the waitlist.

## Highlights

- Next.js 16 with the App Router and optimized fonts via `next/font`.
- The form writes emails straight to Firestore with a server timestamp.
- **SEO:** Open Graph, Twitter Cards, JSON-LD structured data, sitemap, robots and web manifest.
- Firebase configuration comes from environment variables and is validated when the app starts.
